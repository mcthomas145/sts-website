Financing and payment

Pay in full, bring your own lender, or ask us for an introduction to a commercial truck lender.

Three ways to pay

1. Cash, wire or check

Pay in full and take the truck once the funds have cleared. You receive a signed bill of sale and we complete the title paperwork.

2. Finance with your own lender

If you already work with a bank, credit union or equipment finance company, use them. We send your lender what they need: the buyer's order, VIN, year, make, model, mileage and photos. The lender is recorded as lienholder on the title.

3. Ask about our preferred lenders

If you do not have a lender, we can connect you with lenders that finance commercial trucks. You apply with the lender directly.

Good to know: Supreme Truck Sales is a dealer, not a lender. Approval, rates, down payment and terms are decided by the lender, and we do not guarantee financing.

What lenders usually ask for

Requirements differ by lender. Having these ready typically speeds things up:

  • Driver's license, and your CDL if you will drive the truck
  • Business name, how long you have been in business, and your USDOT or MC number if you have one
  • Recent bank statements or tax returns
  • The down payment amount you plan to make
  • Proof of insurance on the truck before it leaves the lot

Costs to plan for beyond the price

  • Sales tax. Indiana sales tax applies unless your purchase qualifies for an exemption or the truck will be registered in another state. We complete the Indiana tax form that fits your situation.
  • Title, registration and plates. These are handled in the state where you register the truck.
  • Heavy Vehicle Use Tax. Heavy trucks used on public highways generally owe this federal tax, filed on IRS Form 2290.
  • Insurance. Your lender and your carrier will each have minimum coverage requirements.

This page is general information, not tax or legal advice. Check with your accountant about your own situation.
