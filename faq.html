Questions and answers

How buying a truck from Supreme Truck Sales works.

Where is Supreme Truck Sales located?

We are based in Anderson, Indiana, northeast of Indianapolis. Trucks are shown by appointment, so contact us before you travel and we will have the truck ready for you.

What kind of trucks do you sell?

Used Class 8 semi trucks. Most of our inventory is sleeper and day cab tractors, and we list a trailer from time to time. Every unit on the inventory page shows its photos, specs and unit number.

Can I see and drive a truck before I buy?

Yes. Book an appointment and we will walk the truck with you, start it, and go over the specs and paperwork. Ask about a test drive when you book.

Can my mechanic inspect the truck?

Yes. You are welcome to bring your own mechanic or arrange an independent inspection before you commit. Our buying guide lists what to check on a used Class 8 truck.

How do I reach you?

Call 765.615.3322, email info@ststrucksales.com, or send a message through the contact page. Showings are by appointment, so please contact us before you visit.

How can I pay for a truck?

Three ways: pay with cash, wire or check; finance through your own bank or lender; or ask us to connect you with one of our preferred commercial truck lenders. Supreme Truck Sales is not a lender, and loan approval and terms are set by the lender.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is available, including to buyers outside Indiana. We quote the delivery cost before you commit so the full number is clear.

Are prices listed?

Most trucks show the asking price on the listing. A few say “Call for price”; contact us with the unit number and we will give you the number.

Do your trucks come with a warranty?

Coverage varies by truck. When a truck is sold with a warranty, the terms are shown on its listing and given to you in writing at the sale.

How do I hold a truck?

Contact us with the unit number. A deposit can hold a truck, and the deposit terms are written on a signed deposit receipt before you pay.

What do I need to bring to buy a truck?

A driver's license, proof of insurance for the truck, and your business details, such as your company name and USDOT or MC number if you have them. If you are financing, bring your lender's contact information.

What paperwork do I get?

A signed buyer's order and bill of sale, the title or title application, the Indiana sales tax form that applies to your purchase, and copies of everything you sign.
