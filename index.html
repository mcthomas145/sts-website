Before you buy any used truck

A used Class 8 truck is a major purchase, and the odometer tells only part of the story. Engine hours, idle time, aftertreatment history and maintenance records matter as much as miles.

Our buying guide lists what to check, what to ask the seller, and which costs to plan for beyond the sale price. It applies wherever you buy.

Common questions

Where is Supreme Truck Sales located?

We are based in Anderson, Indiana, northeast of Indianapolis. Trucks are shown by appointment, so contact us before you travel and we will have the truck ready for you.

How can I pay for a truck?

Three ways: pay with cash, wire or check; finance through your own bank or lender; or ask us to connect you with one of our preferred commercial truck lenders. Supreme Truck Sales is not a lender, and loan approval and terms are set by the lender.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is available, including to buyers outside Indiana. We quote the delivery cost before you commit so the full number is clear.
