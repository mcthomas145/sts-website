Delivery and pickup

Pick up your truck in Anderson, or have it delivered. Either way you know the cost before you commit.

Delivery

We can arrange delivery of your truck, including to buyers outside Indiana. Give us the destination city and we will quote the delivery cost, so it is part of the numbers you agree to and not a surprise afterward.

How it works

  • Tell us the unit number and where the truck is going.
  • We give you a delivery quote along with the buyer's order.
  • Once the truck is paid for or funded and insured, we schedule the delivery with you.
  • You sign a delivery receipt when the truck arrives.

Pickup in Anderson

Pickups are by appointment. Anderson is in Madison County, northeast of Indianapolis along Interstate 69. Buyers flying in usually use Indianapolis International Airport, which is roughly an hour away by road.

Bring with you

  • Your driver's license
  • Proof of insurance on the truck
  • Plates or a transit permit valid for your trip home, if your state requires one

Buying from another state

Out-of-state buyers title and register the truck in their home state. Sales tax treatment depends on where the truck will be registered, and we complete the Indiana form that applies. Ask us before you travel so the paperwork is ready.
