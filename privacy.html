Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how Supreme Truck Sales, doing business as STS (“we”, “us”), handles information collected through ststrucksales.com (the “site”). It covers this website only. Paperwork you complete when you buy a truck is handled under the documents you sign at that time.

Information we collect

Information you send us

When you submit an inquiry, contact, financing or delivery form, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address if you give one, your message, and the truck you asked about.

Information collected automatically

  • Internet address. When you submit a form, our system records your IP address for a short time to block spam and automated abuse.
  • Server logs. The companies that host this site receive standard technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site, and we do not run third-party analytics on it.

How we use your information

  • To answer your inquiry and contact you by phone, text message or email about it
  • To schedule showings, prepare quotes and complete a sale you ask for
  • To keep records of our dealings with customers
  • To protect the site from spam, fraud and misuse
  • To meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • At your request. If you ask us to connect you with a lender or to arrange delivery, we give that lender or carrier the contact and purchase details they need.
  • Service providers. Companies that host this site and our customer records process information for us. They include Cloudflare, which stores inquiries submitted through the site, GitHub, which hosts the site's pages, and Google, which supplies the fonts the site displays.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, a subpoena or a court order, or when needed to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
  • Business transfer. If the business is sold or merged, customer records may transfer to the new owner.

Phone calls, texts and email

By sending a form on this site, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP to a text or by telling us on a call or by email.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry records for as long as they are useful for responding to you and for our business records, and longer where the law requires it.

Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information you sent through this site. Contact us using the details below. We may need to keep some records to meet legal obligations. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold, including encrypted connections and password-protected access to customer records. No website or system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information sent over the internet. Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank details or card numbers through the forms on this site.

Children

This site is intended for adults buying commercial trucks. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

This site may link to other websites, such as a lender's. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
